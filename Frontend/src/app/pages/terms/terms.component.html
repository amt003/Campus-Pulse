2. Description of Service

CampusPulse is a campus placement automation platform designed for students, recruiters, and Training & Placement Officers (TPOs). The Platform facilitates job drive postings, applications, automated eligibility checks, candidate shortlisting, round scheduling, AI resume match scoring, and official job offer generation.

3. Institutional Placement Policy & Placement Freeze

📌 One-Student-One-Offer Rule & Freeze Regulations:

  • Offer Acceptance: When a candidate accepts an official job offer or offer letter on the Platform, their status is updated to "Placed 🎉 (Offer Accepted)".
  • Placement Freeze: Upon offer acceptance, the student's participation in all other active recruitment drives, upcoming test rounds, group discussions, and interview schedules is immediately frozen as per institutional placement rules.
  • Application Locking: Placed students are restricted from submitting new drive applications, and recruiters from other active drives will see candidate status as locked to maintain fair distribution of placement opportunities across the campus body.
