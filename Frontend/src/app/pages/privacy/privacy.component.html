Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

CampusPulse ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our placement platform.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal Identification: Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Roll Number.
  • Academic Details: CGPA, Branch, Year of Graduation, Active Backlogs.
  • Professional Data: Resumes (PDF), Skills, Projects, and Work Experience.
  • Account Data: Login credentials (securely hashed), role type (Student/Recruiter/TPO).
  • Usage Data: IP addresses, browser type, pages visited (for analytics and improving user experience).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify your eligibility for job drives.
  • To match your resume with job descriptions using AI (Sentence-BERT).
  • To facilitate communication between students, recruiters, and TPOs.
  • To provide analytics dashboards to TPOs (aggregated/statistical data, not individual profiling).
  • To send automated email reminders about interviews and placement updates.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

  • Recruiters: Your name, CGPA, resume, and other application details are shared with recruiters only when you apply to their specific job drive.
  • TPOs: The TPO has administrative access to oversee the entire placement process.
  • Third-Party Services: We do not sell or rent your personal data to any third party. We may use trusted cloud services (MongoDB Atlas, Render) to host the application, but your data remains confidential.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Your data is stored in a secure MongoDB database (hosted on MongoDB Atlas).
  • Resumes and offer letters are stored in GridFS, which is part of your MongoDB cluster.
  • Passwords are encrypted using bcrypt.
  • We implement role-based access control (JWT) to ensure only authorized users access specific data.

6. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data (subject to retention for historical placement records).

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies to manage session logins (JWT tokens). We do not use third-party tracking cookies for advertising.

8. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about your privacy, please reach out to tpo@alphabetcollege.edu.in.
